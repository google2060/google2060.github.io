Welcome to LeadsAfrica. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

We may collect the following types of information:

b. Account & Usage Data

  • Login activity

c. Device Information
